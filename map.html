Map of Lorandal

A chart of the continent of Lorandal and its surrounding seas: the Kingdom of Zeref at its heart, the Fey Wood to the northwest, the Greylands behind the Iron Wall, and the open waters of The Veil, the Storm Sea, the Dragon Sea, and the Southern Reach beyond.

Regions of Lorandal​

The continent divides into a handful of broad regions. Below is a guide to each, and what lies within it. For distances between them, see Travel and Distances; for the full list of towns, see Settlements.

The Heartlands​

The core of the Kingdom, strung along the King's River as it runs south from Crown Lake to the sea. This is the realm's political and commercial spine: the capital at Azriel on Crown Lake, the great bridge-city of Varenholm, and the self-governing town of Millhaven, with smaller river towns such as Redcross and Crossfen between them. Barges run the river day and night, and the corridor's cities raise most of the Kingdom's coin. The worked timberland of the Grey Wood runs along the corridor's western edge.

The West Coast​

Where the Kingdom meets the open sea. Karth, the busiest port in the realm, sits on Golden Harbor and takes in most of the traffic arriving by water, including the single elven trade ship that comes each year from Arendale. Beyond its walls lie the Harbor Plains that feed the city.

The South Coast​

The warm, wealthy shore where the Southern Reach comes to Lorandal. Tidefall is the cosmopolitan gateway for southern trade, and Saltmere, west along the coast, is the home port of the Kingdom's fleet. East of Tidefall lies the Dragon Sea, feared and little sailed.

The Eastern Frontier​

The Kingdom's mining country, in the foothills and passes of the World Spine. Ironhold, the only dwarven city, works the mountains alongside the gnome engineers of Coppergate, while the garrison at East Gate watches the eastern trade road and the trails beyond. The Royal Hills rise between here and the capital. Sunfall sits on Mirror Lake at the region's western edge, the farming village of Harrow lies south toward the Southrun, and the old-growth Ashwood spreads between them.

The North​

Stormhaven stands alone on the Storm Sea, the Kingdom's only northern port and its summer link to Kethara. The road north from Azriel runs through Crestfall to reach it. For half the year winter closes the sea, and the city turns in on itself.

The Ash Fields​

A wide blighted waste near the center of the Kingdom where nothing grows, and nothing has in living memory. Farmers work its edges out of necessity and shun its heart, and the ruined places within it, chief among them the dead city of Valdris, which the crown forbids anyone to enter, are left well alone. See Landmarks and Wild Places.

The Fey Wood​

The elven northwest: an ancient forest that resists mapping and rearranges its own paths. The Kingdom knows only its fringe, the trade town of Arendale, open one day a year, and the names of settlements deeper in, such as the capital Moonveil, that no outsider has seen. At the forest's heart stands the Elder Tree, the elves' most sacred site, which no outsider has laid eyes on. On the Kingdom's side of the treeline, the garrison at Mist Watch keeps watch over the Mist Gate. The mist-wrapped coast of The Veil borders the wood to the west. See The Fey Wilds.

The Greylands​

Harsh, poor country behind the Iron Wall in the southwest, held by the clans of Grimhold. Two passes, Iron Pass in the north and Stone Pass in the south, are the only ways through the mountains, and the Kingdom's garrisons have held them against Greylands raids for three thousand years. See The Greylands.

The Surrounding Seas​

Four seas ring the continent. The Veil, off the elven coast to the west, is calm but wrapped in permanent mist. The Storm Sea to the north is dangerous the year round. The Dragon Sea to the east is feared and little sailed, and holds a lone volcanic island. Only the Southern Reach itself is reliably calm, and it carries most of the Kingdom's long-distance trade. Closer in, Golden Harbor shelters Karth in the west, while Irondeep Bay, walled off by mountains in the northeast, sees no honest traffic.

Beyond Lorandal​

East across the Southern Reach lies Kethara, the six-race realm the departing dwarves helped found long ago. Far to the west lies a barren, unmapped continent that explorers do not return from.
