Rivers and Roads

This page lists the named rivers of Lorandal, the Kingdom's road network, and its major trade routes.

Named Rivers​

RiverCourseNotes
The King's RiverCrown Lake to the southern coastThe kingdom's primary artery. Fully navigable, with barges running continuously.
The Goldrun RiverHigh Pool to the King's River near MillhavenNamed for its gold-tan mineral color.
The Spineback RiverThe World Spine to the King's RiverPasses East Gate and Coppergate.
The Southrun RiverThe central plains to the southern coastForms Saltmere Fen at its mouth.
The Marketh RiverThe western plains to the King's RiverMeets it near Crossfen.
The Gold Leaf RiverThe Fey Wilds interior to The VeilRuns past the elven council seat of Gold Leaf.
The Fey RiverSomewhere in the deep Fey Wilds to The VeilSacred to the elves. Its source has never been charted.
The Blood RiverThe Iron Wall to The VeilRuns through Greylands territory; Grimhold sits on its banks.
The East RunThe World Spine to the Storm SeaNamed, not entirely kindly, for the dwarven exodus east three thousand years ago.

The Road Network​

RoadRoutePurpose
The King's RoadAzriel, Millhaven, Varenholm, Redcross, Crossfen, Saltmere, TidefallThe best-maintained road in the kingdom, following the King's River
The Crown RoadAzriel, Ironhold, Sunfall, Coppergate, East GateCapital to the eastern frontier
The Northern RoadAzriel, Crestfall, StormhavenCapital to the northern port
The Mist RoadAzriel, Crestfall, Ashford, Mist WatchA military road only, not used for trade
The Harbor RoadKarth, north of the Ash Fields, VarenholmThe most common early route out of Karth
The Southern Coast RoadKarth, Crossfen, Saltmere, TidefallA coastal trade route past small fishing villages
The Pass RoadsShort stubs to Iron Pass and Stone PassMilitary access only

Major Trade Routes​

RoutePortsGoods
The Kethara RunStormhaven (summer) or Tidefall (winter) to KetharaMetals, precision instruments, dwarven craftsmanship
The Southern CircuitKarth to Tidefall to SaltmereInternal kingdom distribution
The Southern Reach RunTidefall and Saltmere to the southern islandsSpices, tropical goods, luxury materials
The Elven ExchangeKarth to Arendale, once a yearElven crafts and medicinal plants for metals and grain
