History of the Kingdom

The Kingdom of Zeref was founded three thousand years ago and has been ruled by a single unbroken royal line ever since. Most of what happened before and during the founding era has been lost; the record below is what the Kingdom itself teaches and keeps.

The founding​

According to the Kingdom's histories, a darkness rose in the age before the founding and nearly consumed the world. King Zeref led the peoples of Lorandal against it and broke it, and founded the Kingdom on the strength of that victory. He named it after himself while still alive, and is remembered as its founding hero. Founding Day, held each spring, still honors him.

What the darkness actually was, no surviving record says. The oldest accounts are fragmentary or contradictory, and three thousand years of retelling have worn the story into legend. The blighted expanse of the Ash Fields is popularly believed to date from that era, though no one can say for certain what happened there.

The Kingdom's first years also saw two departures whose reasons are equally lost. The elves withdrew, or were driven, into the Fey Wood, and the law separating them from the Kingdom dates from that time and has never been repealed. The greater part of the dwarves left Lorandal entirely, crossing the eastern sea to found what became Kethara. The elves are said to remember an ancient betrayal from this period, but few alive, on either side of the border, could say what it truly was.

The elves and the Kingdom​

For most of the Kingdom's history there was no contact with the Fey Wood at all. The old hostility has thawed only in recent centuries: rare elves are now permitted among men, and the annual trade between Karth and Arendale is the one formal link between the two peoples. See Factions of Lorandal.
