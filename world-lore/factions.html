Factions of Lorandal

Three powers divide the continent of Lorandal: the Kingdom of Zeref, which holds nearly all of it; the elves of the Fey Wood in the northwest; and the clans of the Greylands, sealed behind the Iron Wall in the southwest.

The Kingdom of Zeref​

FoundedCrown Year 0, by King Zeref
TerritoryAll of Lorandal except the Fey Wood
Population~5.2 million
GovernmentHereditary monarchy with crown-appointed governors
Military~52,000 standing

The Kingdom of Zeref has been ruled by a single hereditary human bloodline for three thousand years. There is no hereditary nobility below the crown; every city and town answers to a crown-appointed governor. The King's court is organized around five council seats, one for each of the founding races except the elves, with a single Crown Mage above them all who answers only to the monarch. See King Aldric Zeref and The Council of Azriel.

Magic is unregulated throughout the Kingdom and treated as a skilled trade. Roughly one person in five can manage simple cantrip-level effects.

The cities along the King's River, from Azriel south through Varenholm, Millhaven, Redcross, Tidefall, and Saltmere, generate most of the Kingdom's commercial revenue and are known for saying so.

The elves of the Fey Wood​

The elves have lived apart in the Fey Wood since the Kingdom's earliest years. The law separating the two peoples is three thousand years old, and the reason it was made has been lost on both sides of the border; the elves are said to remember an ancient betrayal, but few alive could say what it truly was. Almost nothing about elven society is known in the Kingdom with certainty.

SettlementWhat is known
MoonveilThe elven capital. Known by name only; no outsider has seen it.
Gold LeafThe seat of the elven Council of Elders. Known mainly through the river that carries its name out of the forest.
ArendaleA coastal trade town, and the only elven settlement whose existence the Kingdom has confirmed.

The Fey Wood resists mapping, and the elven coast along The Veil is permanently wrapped in mist. How many elves live beyond the treeline is unknown.

Once a year, one elven ship sails from Arendale to Karth and one Kingdom ship sails the reverse route, on a date set jointly by the Crown and the Council of Elders. If either ship fails to return, the agreement is permanently broken. See Arendale.

The Greylands​

TerritoryThe southwest peninsula, behind the Iron Wall
PeoplesHobgoblins, bugbears, gnolls, lizardfolk
GovernmentA council of clan leaders at Grimhold

The peoples of the Greylands were pushed behind the Iron Wall when the Kingdom's borders were drawn, and the land they hold has grown poorer every generation since. Raids across the two mountain passes, Iron Pass in the north and Stone Pass in the south, are a fact of life on the frontier; the pass garrisons have held against them for three thousand years. Reports from the garrisons describe four clans, governed by a council whose members seldom pull the same way. See The Greylands for the region, its stronghold at Grimhold, and the passes.
