The Pantheon of Lorandal

The peoples of Lorandal keep a broad and living faith. Each of the founding races honors its own creator god, and above and alongside them stands a wider pantheon worshipped across the whole Kingdom of Zeref.

The creator gods​

Each founding race was made, by its own tradition, in the image of its god, and those gods are still honored today.

The wider pantheon​

These gods are honored across the Kingdom, by every race.

PelorSun, life, healingA radiant sun diskThe Kingdom's dominant healing god. Most temples are Pelor-primary, and his clerics are the most common divine callers in the realm.

Maritha, the Anchored TideLaw, protection, the seaAn anchor embraced by a cresting waveA regional goddess of the southern coast, worshipped by the Tritons of Saltmere, the coastal noble houses, and naval knights.
Sehanine MoonbowThe moon, illusion, secretsA crescent moonThe elven moon goddess, also honored in halfling communities.

The blue moon​

The great Blue Moon Festival is bound up with faith. Kingdom folk have credited Lyssa with the omen since a royal decree made it official centuries ago. The elves credit their own moon goddess, Sehanine Moonbow, instead. Both beliefs are old and sincerely held. See The Blue Moon Festival.
